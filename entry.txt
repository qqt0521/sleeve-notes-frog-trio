Pond Standard Time

Three frogs, one smoky room. Milo blows tenor like he's arguing with the rain, Bea walks the bass low and slow enough to sway the lily pads, and Pip keeps time on a snare made from a tin lid. Recorded live at the Cattail Club — no overdubs, just a band that learned to swing by listening to the pond at night. By the last track, even the heron at the bar is nodding along.
